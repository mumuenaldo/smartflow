About SmartFlow

Digitizing the TelOne Registry Department, one workflow at a time.

What is SmartFlow?

SmartFlow is a web‑based workflow automation system designed specifically for the TelOne Registry Department. It replaces manual paper‑based logbooks with a digital platform that tracks documents in real time, automates approval chains, and maintains a complete audit trail.

The system is built to address the three core challenges identified at the Registry Department:

  • Process Delays — Documents no longer need to be physically moved between offices.
  • Lack of Transparency — Any authorised user can instantly see where a document is in the workflow.
  • Risk of Loss — Digital records are never misplaced or damaged.
